ABOUT THIS PROJECT

This portfolio explores three CSS animations focused on light, motion, and perception. Each page experiments with a different visual idea: flicker, bounce, and optical art (OP ART). The goal was to create distinct environments while keeping the overall design minimal and cohesive.

FLICKER

This animation explores flicker through irregular changes in opacity. I took heavy inspiration from retro neon signs (e.g. motels, diners, etc.) and the NEON film studio logo. To sell the image of a neon sign, I chose to use a vivid, hot pink. Additionally, I made the rest of the background black/dark to make the "sign" stand out. The animation loops infinitely, and the uneven timing helps it feel less mechanical. I wanted to create something slightly unstable and realistic.

BOUNCE

This animation focuses on vertical movement and repetition. I animated three circular forms with staggered timing so the motion feels rhythmic instead of identical. For this, I took inspiration from loading screens and bouncy balls. I used vibrant colors and gradients to make them look like rubber balls. To go along with the playfulness of the theme, I chose a colorful, yet blurred image to place as the background. The repetitive motion of the bouncing reminded me of a loading screen, so I wanted to incorporate those two elements together.

OP ART

This animation uses overlapping striped patterns to create a visual illusion. I kept it black and white so the contrast feels strong and the movement is easier to notice. Instead of using a lot of motion, I focused on rotation and how the two layers interact with each other. The circles being made up of vertical lines made the interactions more dynamic, almost like a puzzle. In terms of design, I wanted the whole page to look minimalistic, to place focus on the illusion.
